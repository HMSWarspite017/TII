## Image processing

Downloaded JPEGs: 2800 × 4200 px each. Full-size local WebP images retain that
resolution, with 800w and 1400w derivatives for responsive loading.
Only mild exposure/saturation adjustments, resizing and WebP encoding were applied.
No AI generation, compositing, removal of objects or invented roots were used.
Display cropping uses CSS object-fit and separately checked desktop/mobile focal positions.
All page images load from local files; no stock-provider requests are needed at runtime.

Approach shows a real soil bed, lower tomato foliage and a support trellis;
it has no visible drip line. Journey photography represents stage groups, rather
than documenting each individual developmental event. No hand harvesting is shown.
